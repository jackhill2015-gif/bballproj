# jack's answers — Campus Dynasty player Q&A (2026-10-02)

Answered directly by jack, a real Campus Dynasty player. Screenshots: `jack-gamecast.jpg`, `jack-dashboard.jpg`.

## Depth chart (chat Q3)
**Editable, but very limited.** (Confirms the reviewer complaint — position changes barely re-slot players.)

## Getting fired (chat Q4)
**Yes — fired a few times.** After being fired you get an option to try for other jobs, each showing a **% chance of landing the job**. You click the school and it tells you if you got it or not. **Higher prestige = better odds at good jobs.** It lines up with real life: a fired low-conference coach has next-to-nothing chance at UCLA.

## Scheme names (from jack-schemes-offense.jpg / jack-schemes-defense.jpg)
Strategy screen has three tabs: **Schemes / Rotation / Usage**.

Offensive schemes:
- **Balanced** — "Balance offensive attack"
- **Motion** — "Utilize everyone to create open shots" (jack's pick)
- **Drive** — "Run through your point guard"
- **Set** — "Slow-paced, post-focused"
- **Early** — "Fast-paced, perimeter focused"

Defensive schemes:
- **Man-to-Man** — "Traditional man-to-man"
- **2-3 Zone** — "Eliminate inside threats" (jack's pick)
- **3-2 Zone** — "Eliminate outside threats"
- **1-3-1 Zone** — "Pressure guards"
- **Box-and-One** — "Lock down opposing star"

Each scheme is a selectable row with name + one-line description and a blue check on the active one. Clean card layout — good reference for architect.
